## Equipment Preparation

### Update Software
Ensure all the following databases and applications are up to date
> [!CAUTION]
> Foreflight Databases and application must be up to date on **BOTH iPhone and iPad**

- Foreflight Databases up to date
- Foreflight App up to date



### Put to Charge
Ensure all the following equipment is put to charge
- Garmin InReach
- Battery Bank
- Headlamp/Flashlight
- iPad
- Phone
- Handheld Radio Batteries
