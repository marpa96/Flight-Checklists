# Short Field Landing

**Owner:** Pablo Martinez
**Applies to:** C172S, VFR, IFR
**Revision:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15
**Source of truth:** [C172S POH § 4](https://www.befa.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/POH-Cessna-172S.pdf)

---
- [ ] Airspeed -- 65 - 75 KIAS (flaps UP)
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- FULL DOWN (30°)
- [ ] Airspeed -- 61 KIAS until flare
- [ ] Power -- REDUCE to idle after clearing obstacle
- [ ] Touchdown -- MAIN WHEELS FIRST
- [ ] Brakes -- APPLY HEAVILY
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- RETRACT
