# Balked Landing

**Owner:** Pablo Martinez
**Applies to:** C172S, VFR, IFR
**Revision:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15
**Source of truth:** [C172S POH § 4](https://www.befa.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/POH-Cessna-172S.pdf)

---
- [ ] Throttle -- FULL OPEN
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- RETRACT TO 20°
- [ ] Climb Speed -- 60 KIAS
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- 10° until obstacles cleared
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- RETRACT after safe altitude and 65 KIAS
