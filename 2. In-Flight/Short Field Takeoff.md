# Short Field Takeoff

**Owner:** Pablo Martinez
**Applies to:** C172R, VFR, IFR
**Revision:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15
**Source of truth:** [C172R POH § 4, p. 4-16](https://kerrville.cap.gov/media/cms/Cessna_172R_POH_712F3D6CCB3C7.pdf)

---
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- 10°
- [ ] Brakes -- APPLY
- [ ] Throttle -- FULL OPEN
- [ ] Mixture -- RICH (above 3000 ft: LEAN for maximum RPM)
- [ ] Brakes -- RELEASE
- [ ] Elevator Control -- SLIGHTLY TAIL LOW
- [ ] Climb Speed -- 56 KIAS until obstacles cleared
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- RETRACT slowly after 60 KIAS
