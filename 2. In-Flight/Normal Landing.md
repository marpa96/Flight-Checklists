# Normal Landing

**Owner:** Pablo Martinez
**Applies to:** C172R, VFR, IFR
**Revision:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15
**Source of truth:** [C172R POH § 4, p. 4-17](https://kerrville.cap.gov/media/cms/Cessna_172R_POH_712F3D6CCB3C7.pdf)

---
- [ ] Airspeed -- 65 - 75 KIAS (flaps UP)
- [ ] Wing Flaps -- AS DESIRED (0° to 10° below 110 KIAS; 10° to 30° below 85 KIAS)
- [ ] Airspeed -- 60 - 70 KIAS (flaps DOWN)
- [ ] Touchdown -- MAIN WHEELS FIRST
- [ ] Landing Roll -- LOWER NOSE WHEEL GENTLY
- [ ] Braking -- MINIMUM REQUIRED
