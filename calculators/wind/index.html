How this estimate works

The calculator resolves the reported wind into headwind/tailwind and crosswind components relative to the desired ground track. It then solves the wind triangle for the crab angle required to cancel the crosswind and estimates groundspeed along the selected course.

It assumes steady wind, level vector geometry, and that the entered approach speed approximates true airspeed. Local terrain, wind shear, gust response, aircraft limitations, and pilot technique are not modeled.

Verify calculations independently and use approved aircraft procedures and limitations.
