# Starting Engine (With External Power)

> [!WARNING]
> **UNVERIFIED.** Adapted for the C172S from the Cessna 172R POH. Check every item against the C172S POH for your aircraft before use.

**Owner:** Pablo Martinez
**Applies to:** C172S, VFR, IFR
**Revision:** 0.1 (unverified)
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15
**Source of truth:** [C172S POH § 4](https://www.befa.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/POH-Cessna-172S.pdf)

---
- [ ] Throttle -- OPEN 1/4 INCH
- [ ] Mixture -- IDLE CUTOFF
- [ ] Propeller Area -- CLEAR
- [ ] Master Switch -- OFF
- [ ] External Power -- CONNECT to airplane receptacle
- [ ] Master Switch -- ON
- [ ] Flashing Beacon -- ON

> [!NOTE]
> Warm engine: skip the next three priming items (page 4-13).

- [ ] Auxiliary Fuel Pump Switch -- ON
- [ ] Mixture -- FULL RICH until stable fuel flow (about 3 to 5 seconds), then IDLE CUTOFF
- [ ] Auxiliary Fuel Pump -- OFF
- [ ] Ignition Switch -- START; release when engine starts
- [ ] Mixture -- ADVANCE smoothly to RICH when engine starts

> [!NOTE]
> Flooded engine: see the procedure note on page 4-13.

- [ ] Oil Pressure -- CHECK
- [ ] External Power -- DISCONNECT; secure external power door
- [ ] Electrical System -- CHECK
  - [ ] Master Switch -- OFF
  - [ ] Taxi and Landing Light Switches -- ON
  - [ ] Engine RPM -- REDUCE to idle
  - [ ] Master Switch -- ON; ammeter shows discharge
  - [ ] Engine RPM -- INCREASE to about 1500 RPM
  - [ ] Ammeter and Low Voltage Annunciator -- ammeter shows charge; VOLTS annunciator OUT

> [!CAUTION]
> If those indications are not seen, the electrical system needs maintenance before flight (page 4-14).

- [ ] Navigation Lights -- ON as required
- [ ] Avionics Master Switch -- ON
- [ ] Radios -- ON
- [ ] Flaps -- RETRACT
