## Personal Equipment Preparation

### Update Software
Ensure all the following databases and applications are up to date
#### iPad
- Foreflight Databases up to date
- Foreflight App up to date
#### iPhone
- Foreflight Databases up to date
- Foreflight App up to date


### Put to Charge
Ensure allt he following equipment is put to charge
- Garmin InReach
- Battery Bank
- Headlamp/Flashlight
- iPad
- Phone
- Handheld Radio Batteries
  
### Kneeboard
- Pens x 3 → Ensure All working
- Earplugs → In Pocket

### Flight Bag
- USB-B to USB-C Cable
- USB-C to USB-C Cable
- iPad Mount
- Apple Pencil is in iPad Case
- Inspect Headset and place in flight bag
  
### Safety Equipment
- Ensure Sentry Connected to Foreflight
- Sentry in Flight Bag
- Sentry Mount in Flight Bag
- Gloves → In flight bag

### Recording Equipment (Optional)
- GoPro → Put to charge
- Go Pro SD Card → Cleared and Re-installed
- Go Pro GA Cable → Put in flight bag
